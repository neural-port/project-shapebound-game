    Hexomino Shape Guide

    There are 35 unique free hexominoes. Rotations and reflections count as the same shape. A win is any 6 of your pieces forming one of these.

    Game Lab

    Experimental configuration. Production games default to 6×6, hexominoes, MAX_ACTIVE_PIECES = 6.

    
    
    
  
    

  
  
    
    
    
      
    
        
    How to Play
    
        
      
    
      
    
        
    
          
    Objective
    
          
    Be the first to form exactly six of your pieces into one of the 35 hexomino shapes — anywhere on the 6×6 board. Don't make a line. Make a shape.
    
        
    
        
    
          
    On Your Turn
    
          
    Click any empty cell to place your symbol. Players alternate: X moves first, then O.
    
        
    
        
    
          
    Winning Shapes
    
          
    A win is any six orthogonally connected cells (up, down, left, right — no diagonals) matching one of the 35 hexominoes (H01–H35). Rotations and reflections count as the same shape. Your winning shape can appear anywhere on the board.
    
        
    
        
    
          
    Piece Lifetime
    
          
    Each player keeps up to 6 active pieces. When you place a 7th piece, your oldest piece disappears. The oldest piece of each player shows a dashed warning ring.
    
        
    
        
    
          
    Win Before Expiration
    
          
    If your new piece completes a winning shape, you win immediately — no piece expires on a winning move.
    
        
    
        
    
          
    Draw
    
          
    A game ends in a draw if the same board position repeats 3 times, or after 200 turns.
    
        
    
        
    
          
    Modes
    
          
    Human vs Human — two players, one device. Human vs AI — play against the computer (Easy / Normal / Hard). Practice — play against the AI without affecting your match score.
